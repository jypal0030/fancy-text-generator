How to use kaomoji

Search for a mood like “happy” or a reaction like “shrug,” then click a face to copy it. For example, add (＾▽＾) to a cheerful greeting or use ¯\_(ツ)_/¯ as a standalone reply.

Kaomoji are built from text characters. Check your message after pasting: spacing and symbols can look different depending on the receiving app and its font.
