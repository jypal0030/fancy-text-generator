Fraktur, blackletter and Gothic text

Blackletter is a family of lettering styles often called Gothic. Fraktur is one style in that family. Regular Fraktur has lighter strokes; bold Fraktur gives a short heading more weight. The three framed previews reuse those letterforms and add brackets or diamonds around the text.

  • Regular: Dark → 𝔇𝔞𝔯𝔨 for a short display name.
  • Bold: Dark → 𝕯𝖆𝖗𝖐 for a decorative heading.

This tool replaces A–Z letters with Unicode characters; numbers, accented letters and punctuation stay as typed. No font installation is needed. Check the result after pasting, since some apps may reject these characters or show empty boxes. Keep important information in plain text for readability and screen readers.
