How to use styled text in your bio

Copy the style you like, open Instagram’s Edit Profile screen, and paste it into the Bio field. Check the result before saving: Unicode letters can look different across devices, and Instagram’s own field limit is what counts.

Keep important details like your name, links and contact information in ordinary text. Try styling one short phrase so the rest stays easy to read and search. To compare more weights and letter shapes, use the Bold Font Generator.
