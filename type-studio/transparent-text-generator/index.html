Transparent Text Generator

Make a PNG from a word or phrase and place it over an image, slide or banner without a solid background.

Will the background appear in my download?

No. The tiled backdrop is only a preview aid. The PNG contains the lettering and transparent space. Padding adds clear space around the letters, and line breaks create separate lines in the image.
