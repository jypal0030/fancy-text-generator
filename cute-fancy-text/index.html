Which cute text style should I use?

Hearts, stars and bows frame your text without changing the letters, so they’re a good choice for names or words with accents. Sweet script also replaces A–Z letters with Unicode cursive characters.

Paste your result into your bio, caption or message and check how it looks before posting. Some apps display decorative characters differently, and screen readers may not pronounce styled letters like ordinary text.
