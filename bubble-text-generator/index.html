What changes in bubble text?

Outlined letters preserve uppercase and lowercase, as in Ⓐ and ⓐ. Filled letters use one uppercase-looking form for both. Spaces and punctuation remain unchanged, and letters outside A–Z are kept as typed.

Try styling just one short word in a caption. Some devices display enclosed characters differently, so check how your text looks after pasting.

  • Outlined: Hi 2! → Ⓗⓘ ②!. The lowercase i stays lowercase.
  • Filled: Hi 2! → 🅗🅘 ❷!. Both letters take an uppercase-looking form.
