Brackets or boxed letters?

Brackets frame the full entry without changing the letters inside. Boxed styles convert A–Z letters to uppercase-looking Unicode squares and leave numbers, spaces, punctuation and accented letters as typed.

  • Corner brackets: Hello → 「Hello」 to frame a greeting without changing the letters.
  • Outlined squares: Hello → 🄷🄴🄻🄻🄾 to put each letter in its own box.

These are Unicode characters, not a font you install. Some apps may render squares differently or show missing characters. If that happens, try a bracket style and check the result after pasting.
