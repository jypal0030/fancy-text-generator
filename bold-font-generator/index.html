Which bold style should I copy?

  • Bold serif: 𝐒𝐭𝐮𝐝𝐢𝐨 𝟐𝟒 gives a display name a traditional look.
  • Bold sans serif: 𝗡𝗲𝘄 𝘁𝗼𝗱𝗮𝘆 makes a clean caption opener.
  • Bold italic serif: 𝑵𝒆𝒘 𝒕𝒐𝒅𝒂𝒚 adds slanted emphasis; digits stay unchanged in this style.

These styles use Unicode characters, not rich-text formatting. They convert A–Z letters and leave punctuation and accented letters as typed. If your app offers native bold formatting, use that for longer text: it keeps the underlying letters unchanged for search and assistive technology.
