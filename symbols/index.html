How to use copied symbols

Decorate a display name with ✦ Luna, separate two interests with Art ♡ Music, or introduce a link with Details →. If a symbol carries meaning, use words alongside it so readers don’t have to guess.

For an equation, choose the maths character rather than a lookalike. For example, × is a multiplication sign, not the letter x or the emoji ❌.

These are Unicode characters rather than images. Their appearance depends on the font and app, so check them after pasting. To transform whole words instead of adding individual symbols, try the Fancy Text Generator.
