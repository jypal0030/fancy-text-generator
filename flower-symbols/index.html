Where to paste flower symbols

  • Instagram: Paste 🌷 Spring sketches into your bio or use it to open a caption.
  • Discord: Put ✿ · ✿ · ✿ on its own line between two parts of a message. For more options, see Discord symbols and dividers.
  • WhatsApp: Paste ❀ Have a lovely day! into a chat, or add a single flower to a message you’ve already written.

Flower symbol FAQ

How do I copy and paste a flower symbol?

Select Copy under a flower, then paste it into your bio, caption or message. If automatic copying is unavailable, select the symbol itself and use your device’s copy command.

What’s the difference between ❀ and 🌸?

❀ is a text ornament that usually takes the surrounding text colour. 🌸 is the cherry blossom emoji, and its artwork can look different across apps and devices.

Why does a flower show up as an empty box?

The font or device displaying your text may not support that character. Try another flower and preview it in the destination app before posting.
